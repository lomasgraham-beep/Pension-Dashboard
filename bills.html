Household Bills

Add, edit or delete bills. Annual £ is the yearly cost today — for Monthly bills it now counts only the months the bill is actually charged. Paid in sets which months that is — tap it to pick (e.g. council tax over 10 months, off in Feb & Mar), and the Annual figure updates to match. Reduce? ties the bill to the retirement spending slider. Taper 70/80/90 is the % of the cost retained at each age (1 = full, 0.5 = half).Renewal is the policy renewal date for insurances — when set, that insurance shows on the Budget page only in its renewal month. The darker Holidays, Dining, Pensions, Loans, Xmas and Birthdays lines are drawn from their pages — read-only here and counted towards the yearly total, but not saved as bills. Pensions shows this year’s contributions at each member’s current working-days tier, so it changes automatically when a tier changes (e.g. a 3-day week from September). Xmas and Birthdays come from the Gift Savings page (value × age taper). (bills build: pens4)
