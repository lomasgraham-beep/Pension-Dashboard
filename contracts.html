Contracts

Every dated commitment in one register — insurance, MOT, road tax, servicing, storage. End Date is what drives the reminders: an email goes out 28, 14, 7 and 1 day before, and again on the day, for any row with Notify ticked. The Reminders chips light up as each one is sent — tap a chip to clear it (so it sends again) or to light it manually (so it does not). Renew moves both dates on by a year and clears the chips ready for the new term. Changing the End Date clears them automatically. Suppliers are held in their own list — open Suppliers to add a phone number or website, which then travels with every contract that uses it.
