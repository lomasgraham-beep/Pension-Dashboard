Cost Analysis (costs v3)

Actual spending per category, from your card transactions. Bars run highest cost (left) to lowest (right). Switch between a single month, a calendar year, or a rolling 12 months, and drag the zoom to widen the bars. In a year view the table also shows the monthly average — handy for variable costs like groceries and fuel. Anything categorised Reimburse is excluded.
