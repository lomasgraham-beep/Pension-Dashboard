Fill in descriptions from an Amazon export

Amazon → Account → Data & Privacy → Request My Data → Your Orders. Unzip what arrives and pick Retail.OrderHistory.1.csv below. The file is read here in the browser and nothing from it is stored — only the product names it can match to a charge are offered, and only as suggestions you then save.

Google Sheets round-trip

Copy the rows above, paste them into your sheet at A2, fill in the last two columns, then copy the block back and paste it below. Column order is fixed: Ref, Date, Amount, Statement text, What was it?, Category. Never change the Ref column — it is the only thing tying a row back to its transaction.

Setting the sheet up, once

  1. New Google Sheet. Press Copy headings here and paste at A1.
  2. Add a second tab called Categories. Press Copy category list and paste it at A1 of that tab.
  3. On the first tab select F2:F1000, then Data → Data validation → Add rule → Dropdown (from a range) → Categories!A:A.

That validation belongs to your sheet, so it survives every future paste. Come back each month, paste new rows at A2, and the dropdown is already there.
