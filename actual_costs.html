Actual Costs

Categories driven by your real card spend instead of their planned bill rows. Each row takes the last 12 months of transactions in that category and divides by 12. When Use actual is ticked, that category is driven by this figure across the budget, the bills summary and the whole retirement model — your plan is left untouched in the database, so unticking restores it exactly. The figure is recalculated every time it is read, so it always matches the transactions behind it. Adjust is an annual amount added to (or, if negative, taken off) the card total before it is used — for a category that is part cash, carries a cost with no card trail, or contains a one-off you do not want projected forward. The adjusted total can never go below zero. (actual costs build: v4)
