Actual Costs

Categories driven by your real card spend instead of their planned bill rows. Each row takes the last 12 months of transactions in that category and divides by 12. When Override is ticked, every bill in that category is replaced by this figure across the budget, the bills summary and the whole retirement model — your planned bills are left untouched in the database, so unticking restores them exactly. The figure is recalculated every time it is read, so it always matches the transactions behind it. (actual costs build: v2)
