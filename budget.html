Monthly Budget

Per-person budget for the period you're viewing: net income in, the bills that leave each account, and what's left. Use the Monthly / Annual toggle to switch between the viewed month and the whole-year total; in monthly view, step to next month or jump ahead, and the muted avg figure is the smoothed 12-month monthly cost. PayPal shows as one line in the account it draws from; pensions are gathered into their own block and taken off once at the end. The working-days chip at the top of each person’s card is read-only and follows the schedule in Basic Data against the month you’re viewing — so a 3-day week starting in September shows 5-day in August and 3-day from September, and salary and pension contributions switch with it. Figures are shown in the money of the year you're viewing: bills, income, insurances and savings set-asides grow with inflation (2.5%/yr), stepping once each January rather than creeping every month, so amounts hold steady across a year. Pension contributions and loan payments follow their own dated schedules and are not inflated on top. The Credit Card box shows the Groceries, Fuel, Julie Hair & Nails and Dogs spend, which is deducted in the Cash Summary. Hair & Nails follows the same Plan / Actual basis as dining, holidays and fuel (set on the Weekly Plan page): on Actual it uses your real card spend on the JULIE category over the last 12 months, divided by 12, and the row is marked actual. If that category doesn’t exist or can’t be read it quietly falls back to the planned bill row. Xmas and Birthdays are not on the card here — they are saved monthly into a savings account (age-tapered gift values, inflated 0.5%/yr, managed on the Gift Savings page) and appear as a Gift savings set-aside against that account’s paying account, smoothing the December peak. Insurances are listed in the third column. Loans show as a line in the account they’re paid from, and the Loans card lists what is still owed on each — the balance outstanding at the start of the viewed month and how many payments remain, counting that month. Settled loans drop off the card.
