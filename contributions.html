Every pension contribution as a dated series. Each entry is the monthly amount from its effective date onward, until a later-dated entry takes over — so to plan a change (now or in the future) add an entry with the new amount and the date it starts. The dashboard, budget, forecast and KPI all read the value in force for the month they show. Increase % grows the amount once a year in the increase month; a planned step is flat from its date. Leave Tier as All unless the amount differs by working-days week. Contributions stop at retirement.

Enter what leaves your bank. For a private pension the monthly £ is your actual bank debit; the model adds HMRC relief-at-source (×1.25), so the pot receives 25% more than you pay. For a workplace pension enter the amount deducted (no top-up applied). This lets you reconcile straight against your bank statements.

Contribution Exceptions

Adjust contributions for a period without changing the standard rows above. Each exception has a member, pension, date range and value.

One-off (ticked): a lump sum (e.g. a bonus) added on top of the normal contribution, applied once at the start date. Override (unticked): replaces the normal monthly contribution with this value for every month from start to end.
