Loans (loans build: v2)

Loans and financed purchases. Each row amortises from its start date over the number of payment periods at the interest rate, and the monthly payment is worked out for you. Set the account it’s paid from (that’s where it shows on Bills). Overpay in any month to clear it sooner — the payoff date updates live. A pure loan has no deposit; for a financed purchase, enter the price as the amount and the deposit separately (amount financed = amount − deposit). Loans finishing before your retirement date don’t affect the model; only payments falling on or after retirement do.
