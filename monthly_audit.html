Monthly Audit (audit build: maudit8)

The engine's own month-by-month workings, shown so each row's sums can be checked by eye: Bills + Dining + Holidays = Outgoings, then Gross fixed income − Tax = Net, the Gap that's left, and the pension draw (with its own tax) that fills it. Each row closes with Pot start − Draw + Growth = Pot closing exactly — growth shows red when a market crash is pulling the pot down. The reconciliation line under each year proves these months add up to exactly what the yearly tables show — same engine, same run.
