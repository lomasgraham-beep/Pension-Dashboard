Gift Savings

Xmas and birthday gift budgets, saved into a savings account and spent on the credit card. Each line holds three planned annual amounts — at 70, at 80, at 90 — and the one matching the reference person’s age is used, then inflated 0.5% a year from this year. The lines are summed, divided by 12, and that monthly set-aside shows as the Xmas and Birthdays rows in the Budget’s Credit Card box (and in the other bill views). (gift savings build: v1)
