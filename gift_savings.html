Gift Savings

Xmas and birthday gift budgets, saved into a savings account. Enter the value of each gift line and a taper for ages 70 / 80 / 90 — a fraction from 1 (full, 100%) down to 0 (none), exactly like the Bills taper. Below 70 the full value applies; from 70 the matching taper is used. The tapered values are summed, inflated 0.5%/yr from this year, divided by 12, and that monthly set-aside is what the Budget draws from the savings account. (gift savings build: v2)
