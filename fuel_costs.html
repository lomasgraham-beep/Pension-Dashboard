Fuel rate

Used to turn planned miles into pounds, everywhere in the app.

Fuel = miles ÷ MPG = gallons, × 4.546 = litres, × price per litre. Ticking Towing on any line switches it to the towing MPG.

Commuting

Fuel for work is treated differently from the rest: it is a fixed cost that stops on a date rather than fading with age, and the spending slider does not touch it. Cutting back on luxuries does not shorten the commute.

Leave blank if commuting never stops in the model.

Age taper

How discretionary driving — Holiday, Caravan and Out and about — reduces as you get older, as a multiplier on the older person's age. 1.0 means no reduction. Commuting is never tapered.

Standing weekly driving

Driving you do every week, keyed once here instead of 52 times. Each line appears in every week on the Weekly Plan, greyed out. Change or delete it in a single week there and that week alone detaches — useful for zeroing the commute in a week you were away.
