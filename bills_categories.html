Short codes work best (they appear in the bills table and summary). The description is a friendly label; the definition is longer free text shown at the top of the Household Bills page when you filter to this category.

Edit any description or definition below, then Save. Codes can’t be changed — bills, card transactions and merchant keys all point at them.

Tick Use actual costs to drive a category from your real card spend instead of the planned figure — the trailing 12 months divided by 12. It only takes effect when the basis on the Weekly Plan page is Mixed (or Actual, which additionally forces dining, holidays and fuel on). Card spend (12mo) is your coverage check: a category with no card spend, or with no bill row to override, is flagged here because the tick would otherwise do nothing silently. Categories paid by direct debit — council tax, mortgage — have no card spend and should stay unticked.
